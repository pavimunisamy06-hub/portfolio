CASE STUDY

Inside one project: Flavour Home

How a food ordering app concept went from a problem statement to a clickable prototype.

Flavour Home — ordering homemade food

8 screens · Figma · Mobile, 390 × 844

Concept project
PROBLEM

Ordering homemade food means long phone calls, unclear menus and no way to see what's actually available today.

GOAL

Let someone browse home kitchens, pick a dish and place an order in under a minute.

USER NEEDS

See real dishes and prices, know delivery time up front, and reorder a favourite without starting over.

RESEARCH

Looked at how popular food apps handle browsing and checkout, and noted where the flow felt heavy for a small menu.

WIREFRAMES

Low-fidelity layouts first, testing where the cart, filters and dish details should sit on a small screen.

DESIGN SYSTEM

One type scale, an 8-point spacing grid, a warm accent colour and reusable buttons, cards and input components.

HIGH-FIDELITY UI

Splash, onboarding, login, home, food details, cart, checkout and order confirmation, built with auto layout.

PROTOTYPE

Screens linked into a clickable flow in Figma so the whole ordering journey can be walked through.

OUTCOME

A complete, consistent flow where ordering takes four taps from home screen to confirmation.

LEARNINGS

Components save hours. And a screen only works once you decide what the user should notice first.

Splash→Onboarding→Login→Home→ Food details→Cart→Checkout→Confirmation
